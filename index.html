Program Overview

Deep dive into our curriculum, state-of-the-art labs, and industry ties.

Curriculum Highlights

  • Analog IC Design: Single/two-stage Op-Amps, bandgap references, converters (ADC/DAC).
  • Digital RTL Design: Verilog programming, finite state machines, IP core design.
  • Physical Design & STA: Synthesis, floorplanning, placement, routing, static timing analysis.
  • Emerging Tech: Neuromorphic Computing, AI Hardware, and CMOS VLSI layouts.

Lab Facilities

  • EDA Software: Full suite Cadence Virtuoso, Synopsys Custom Compiler, Mentor Graphics.
  • FPGA Labs: Xilinx Vivado, Intel Quartus, Basys 3 and Artix-7 development hardware.
  • CoE Labs: IIoT Sensor CoE, Intelligent IoT lab, and Maker Village fabrication support.
  • C2S Support: Chip-to-Startup program offering physical tape-out manufacturing opportunities.

Industry Ties

  • TI & Intel Co-Op: Cooperative labs, training, and internship placement setups.
  • Maker Village: India’s largest hardware incubator providing prototype assembly and testing.
  • Industry Internships: Multi-month hardware engineering internships at major semiconductor firms.
  • Expert Seminars: Regular tech talks and mentorship sessions by VLSI industry veterans.
